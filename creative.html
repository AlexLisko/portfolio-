WORK PRESS RESUME ABOUT CREATIVE LAB PHILOSOPHY

Creative Lab & Tactile Craft — Outside of work, I love finding tactile outlets to express pure creativity. Driven by a secondhand-first ethos, every garment is reconstructed from reclaimed, vintage, and recycled materials. From bespoke sewing to experimental painting, bringing that resourceful maker mindset into physical mediums fuels the creative thinking and problem-solving I bring back into product design.
